Accounts

Create accounts, grant roles, and manage every account on the platform.

Grant a role

Look up a signed-up account by email, then promote it to tutor or admin. New accounts start as students until you do this.

Create an account

Creates their login and profile in one step. Leave the password blank to auto-generate a temporary one to share with them.

Account creation takes a few seconds — it creates the sign-in, writes their profile, and sends a welcome email in sequence, so the button doesn't feel instant. That's normal.
