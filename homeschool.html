Curricula we support

South Africa

CAPS

The Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement — South Africa's official national school curriculum, followed by most public and private schools.

South Africa

IEB

The Independent Examinations Board — a well-established South African curriculum and assessment body used by many independent schools.

British Curriculum

Internationally recognised Cambridge-style qualifications, a common choice for families wanting a curriculum that travels well across borders.

How HelloEducation fits in

We work in association with CambriLearn, who provide the accredited homeschool curriculum itself. You enrol in your chosen curriculum directly with them — what HelloEducation adds on top is a qualified tutor who works with your child through that curriculum, subject by subject, so they get real one-on-one guidance rather than working through the material alone.

Who this is for

Grade 1–12 learners already homeschooling on one of the curricula above, or families exploring homeschooling and wanting support choosing a curriculum and getting started with it.

How it works

1

Choose a curriculum

Pick CAPS, IEB, British or American — or tell us if you're not sure yet and need guidance.

2

We match you with a tutor

We pair your child with a tutor experienced in that curriculum and the subjects they need.

3

Learn together, on your terms

Regular sessions, online or face-to-face, that fit around your family's homeschool routine.
