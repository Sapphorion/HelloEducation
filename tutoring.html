What we offer

Grade 1–12

General Tutoring

One-on-one or small-group instruction in the subjects your child is working through right now. Whether it's homework help, filling a gap from a tricky term, or building confidence before tests, we match you with a tutor suited to how your child actually learns.

Focused, exam-driven support for matric students — working through past papers, tightening up exam technique, and targeting the specific topics that are costing marks. Built around your exam timetable, not a generic study plan.
