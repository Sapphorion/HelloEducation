A real second chance at your matric certificate

Never finished matric, or need to rewrite a subject? Our Second Chance Programme gives adult matric students a flexible, structured way to complete their National Senior Certificate — on your schedule, at your pace.

Contact Us to Enrol Login

What we offer

Structure

A Fixed Weekly Timetable

Structured online classes, Monday to Thursday, in small groups of up to four students per subject — real teacher time, not just self-study.

Flexibility

Any NSC Subject You Need

Enrol in exactly what you still need to complete — one subject to rewrite, or a full matric certificate. You're not locked into a whole year of subjects you've already passed.

Convenience

Learn From Anywhere

Classes happen online at a set weekly time, so you always know when to show up — no need to relocate or attend in person to get back on track.

How it works

1

Get in touch

Tell us which subjects you still need to complete or rewrite for your NSC.

2

We enrol you

We place you into a weekly class slot for each subject, alongside a small group of peers.

3

Attend your classes

Join your subjects at their set weekly time, with assessments and progress tracked as you go.

The Second Chance Programme runs separately from our general tutoring — it's a fixed-timetable programme built specifically around what adult matric students need: a real class, a real teacher, and a clear path to a finished certificate.
