About the DBE's Second Chance Matric Programme

"Second Chance" is also the name of a free national programme run by South Africa's Department of Basic Education (DBE), for anyone who needs to rewrite failed subjects, improve their marks, or complete an outstanding National Senior Certificate (NSC) or Senior Certificate (SC).

  • NSC candidates who wrote matric from 2008 onwards, rewriting or improving subjects, or completing an outstanding NSC
  • Senior Certificate (adult) candidates aged 18 and older, writing in June
  • Part-time NSC candidates who previously attempted the exams post-2008, writing in May/June or November

Registering and sitting exams through the DBE's programme is completely free — the DBE explicitly states it does not endorse any private or commercial service that charges for participation in the programme itself. The DBE also provides its own free support: face-to-face classes at learning centres around the country, TV and radio broadcasts, digital courses, past exam papers, and the "Mind the Gap" study guides.

How HelloEducation fits in

We're not affiliated with the DBE, and we don't charge for anything the DBE already provides free. What we offer instead is the teaching layer many students still need on top of that: a dedicated tutor, a small weekly class, and someone tracking your progress subject by subject. You still register for your exams directly with the DBE — we're here to help you actually pass them.
